# Product case study

## The problem

Product decisions rarely fail because the final sentence was not stored. They fail because the evidence, assumptions, follow-ups, and later outcome become separated across notes, tasks, documents, and memory.

The initial product tried to serve general personal productivity. The sharper opportunity was decision memory for product managers and builders: preserve the complete chain from evidence to outcome.

## The wedge

The primary scenario is a product lead handling customer interviews, research, meeting notes, and a decision in flight. Auxiliaire should answer:

- What evidence produced this decision?
- Which assumption is still unverified?
- What is the cleanest next step?
- What changed after the decision?
- Was the forecast calibrated?

## The golden loop

The guided workspace demonstrates an enterprise-pricing question:

1. A meeting note records healthy activation, procurement stalls, and rollout-risk feedback.
2. Auxiliaire proposes a decision, uncertainties, and follow-ups.
3. The user approves a source-linked action to model annual-plan economics.
4. Today ranks the action and explains its urgency.
5. Review returns to the frozen forecast and compares it with the outcome.

## Deliberate product choices

- **Human approval over autonomous organization.** AI prepares proposals; it does not silently move or rewrite memory.
- **Attention budget over an infinite priority list.** Work outside the selected capacity remains visible without pretending it fits.
- **Evidence links over duplicate context.** Actions point back to the capture or decision that created them.
- **Forecasts over hindsight.** Expected outcomes and confidence are recorded before results are known.
- **Contextual research over a separate research product.** Web enrichment lives inside a memory item and preserves earlier snapshots.
- **Honest storage language over security theater.** Browser caching is disclosed as a cache; provider boundaries are visible.

## Rejected directions

- A general-purpose AI chat surface: too easy to reproduce and disconnected from product state.
- A full project-management suite: too broad and team-oriented for the core evidence problem.
- Automatic AI mutations: efficient in demos but harmful to trust in a memory product.
- Five shallow integrations: one PWA share target proves low-friction ingestion without credential-heavy setup.

## Success signals

The product should reduce reconstruction work, not maximize session length. Useful signals include decisions with attached evidence, actions completed with source context, forecasts reviewed after outcomes, and captures clarified without losing raw input.
